What I actually do

I design and ship web things that solve real problems — from tiny everyday utilities to a whole programming language with its own IDE. No frameworks required: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, deployed straight to GitHub Pages.

AI is the co-pilot. It lets me explore ideas that would otherwise take weeks, in an afternoon.
